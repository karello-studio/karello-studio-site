# Privacy Policy for Three Pebbles

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

This policy covers the **Three Pebbles** app (the "App") from **Karello Studio** ("we", "us").

## What we collect

We have no accounts or servers, and the App sends us no personal information, so we don't keep any data from the App. Your game data, such as progress, settings and multiplayer name, stays on your device and can be included in your own device backups.

## Third parties

The App uses services from Google and Apple. They keep and handle data under their own policies, may process it outside your country, and we expect them to protect it to the same standard as this policy.

- **Ads:** Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads. When the App starts and when ads are loaded or shown, Google may process technical information such as your IP address, approximate location, identifiers, device details, diagnostics and ad interactions. Ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalized.
- **Purchases:** the App Store and Google Play handle payments. We never see your payment details.
- **Game Center and Google Play Games:** if you're signed in, your achievements, scores and account name go to Apple or Google, and other players may see them. These services may also collect their own usage data.

Data sent to Google's ad and game services is encrypted in transit. See the [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) privacy policies.

## Nearby multiplayer

Nearby play connects devices directly using Bluetooth, your local network and, on older Android versions, the location permission. The App doesn't read your location. Nearby players can see your display name, and the connection isn't encrypted.

## Your choices

You can change permissions and notifications in your device settings, ad privacy choices in the App's Settings where Google offers them, and your Apple or Google data in those accounts. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes your game data from the device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion and complaining to a regulator. We hold none of it ourselves, but you're welcome to contact us.

## Children

The App doesn't ask anyone's age and treats every player the same way. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## This website

This website has no cookies or analytics of our own. [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) hosts it and may log technical information such as your IP address, and YouTube's trailer loads only when you press play.
