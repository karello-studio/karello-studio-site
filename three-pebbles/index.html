Three Pebbles

Line up three.

A calm two-player strategy game in the tradition of Nine Men's Morris and the old mill board games. Place and slide your pebbles to make a line. Nothing is ever captured: on the bigger boards, a scored line returns to your hand. Play the computer or a friend nearby, no internet needed. Available in 15 languages.

Coming soon to iOS and Android Watch the trailer

Play a friend nearby, no internet

Your phones connect directly over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi, with no account, so it works even with no signal. iPhone and Android can play each other. Or pass one phone between you, or take on the computer at three levels. There's no online play.

Three boards

Classic, Standard and the bigger four-ring Advanced board. Advanced is part of Strategist, and you can try a game by watching an ad.

Puzzles every day

A new Daily Puzzle with a streak to keep, plus Win from Here (three free a day) and Pebble Rush (one free run a day).

Make it yours

Earn boards, pebbles, strikes and themes by playing.
