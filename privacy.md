# Privacy Policy for Three Pebbles

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Three Pebbles** app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by **Karello Studio** (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a freemium app with optional in-app purchases and optional user-triggered rewarded ads. This service is intended for use as is.

## Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect data that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or contact details. Game statistics, preferences, Daily Puzzle streaks, and Puzzle Archive completion/unlock state are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to the Service Provider.

The Application does not collect usage analytics. Crash reporting is **off by default**: no crash data is sent unless you opt in, either by accepting the prompt shown on first launch, or by enabling "Send crash reports" in Settings. You can turn this off again at any time in Settings; crash reports not yet sent are then deleted from your device.

The Application may offer optional, user-triggered rewarded ads, including ads that unlock one older Daily Puzzle Archive challenge for free users. Rewarded ads are not shown automatically during gameplay, and the ad software is not started until you ask to watch one. Each time the Application starts, it contacts Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to check whether a consent form is required in your region and, if so, shows it. This check sends Google technical information such as your IP address and basic device details, whether or not you ever watch an ad. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, the Android Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and related technical information to provide, measure, personalize, and secure ads, subject to your consent choices where required. You can manage available ad privacy choices from the in-app Settings screen.

## Third Party Access

When you have opted in to crash reports, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics when the Application crashes or hits a serious error, so the Service Provider can fix it. A report contains technical information about the error (such as a stack trace), the app version, and device/OS information, along with an installation identifier assigned by Firebase. It does not directly identify you and is not used for advertising.

When you choose to view a rewarded ad, ad-related data may be transmitted to Google AdMob as described above. Ad consent is separate from the Application's crash-reports opt-in.

The Application utilizes the following third-party services which have their own Privacy Policies:

*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   **Google AdMob:** [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Local Connectivity and Permissions

To provide local multiplayer features, looking for nearby players, the Application requires access to:
*   **Bluetooth (BLE) / Nearby Devices:** Used strictly for discovering other devices running Three Pebbles in your immediate vicinity. No location tracking is performed.
*   **Local Network:** Used to communicate with other devices on your same WiFi network.
*   **Location (Android 11 and earlier only):** Those Android versions require this permission for Bluetooth/WiFi device scanning. The Application does not use it to determine your location.

These permissions are used solely for the purpose of establishing a direct connection between devices for gameplay. No personal data is transmitted to the Service Provider via these features.

If you allow notifications, the Application uses them only for the optional daily puzzle reminder, which is scheduled on your device. No data is sent to the Service Provider.

## Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The Application asks the store on your device which purchases you own; nothing about your purchases is sent to the Service Provider, and the Service Provider does not receive your payment details.

## Data Retention

The Application does not store personal data on external servers operated by the Service Provider. Crash data collected by Firebase Crashlytics, ad-related data collected by Google AdMob, and purchase-related data processed by the app stores are retained according to those providers' policies. Local game data (statistics, preferences) remains on your device until you uninstall the Application or clear app data.

## Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider to have the information removed.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
*   **Access:** Request information about data collected about you
*   **Deletion:** Request deletion of your local data by uninstalling the Application or clearing app data; for third-party crash, ads, or purchase data, contact the Service Provider or the relevant third-party provider as applicable
*   **Opt-out:** Crash reporting is off until you consent. You can enable or disable "Send crash reports" at any time from the in-app Settings screen. You can manage available ad privacy choices from Settings as provided by Google AdMob's consent tools.

## Security

The Application operates primarily offline, with game data stored locally on your device. External communication may include opt-in Firebase crash reporting, optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, app store purchase processing, and local multiplayer discovery/connection. Firebase, Google AdMob and the app stores use industry-standard transport security. Local multiplayer connects the two devices directly over Bluetooth or your local WiFi network without going through any server; this connection is not encrypted and carries only game moves, game settings and cosmetic choices. While you host a game, your chosen display name is broadcast so nearby players can find it.
