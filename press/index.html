Descriptions

One line

Three Pebbles is a calm mill strategy game: line up three, outplay the AI, or play a friend nearby.

Short

Three Pebbles is a calm two-player strategy game in the tradition of Nine Men's Morris. Line up three of your pebbles to score a set across three boards, against the computer at three levels or a friend on the same phone or nearby, with no account and no internet needed.

Long

Three Pebbles is a calm strategy game about one simple idea: line up three of your pebbles to score a set. Nothing is ever captured: on the bigger boards, a scored line returns to your hand.

There are three boards: Classic, the quick three-pebble game; Standard, the nine-pebble board people have played for centuries; and Advanced, a bigger four-ring board for longer games, which is part of the Strategist upgrade. Play the computer at Easy, Medium or Hard, pass one phone between you, or connect to a friend nearby over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. The AI runs on the phone and works offline.

A new Daily Puzzle arrives every day, alongside Win from Here and Pebble Rush. Players earn belts, achievements, boards, pebbles and themes by playing. There are no energy timers, loot boxes or in-game currency, no account to make, and no tracking.
