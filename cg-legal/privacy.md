# Privacy Policy for Categrove

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

This policy covers the **Categrove** app (the "App") from **Karello Studio** ("we", "us").

## What we collect

We have no accounts, and we don't keep any data from the App. Your game data, such as your name, settings, Word Shelf and purchases, stays on your device and can be included in your own device backups.

## Word lists

Once a day, the App checks our server, wordsets.karellostudio.com, for new word lists and downloads them. Word lists are downloaded over HTTPS and checked against our signature. The request carries no identifier of you or your device, but Cloudflare, which serves the files, may log technical information such as your IP address. You can turn this off in the App's Settings.

## Purchases

The App Store and Google Play handle payments under their own privacy policies ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). We expect them to protect your data to the same standard as this policy. We never see your payment details.

## Nearby play

Nearby play connects devices directly using Bluetooth and your local network and, on older Android versions, the location permission. The App doesn't read your location. Nearby players, and anyone nearby or on the same network while you host, can see your display name. Players in your room also see your answers and seat style. The connection isn't encrypted.

## Your choices

You can change permissions in your device settings. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes your game data from the device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion and complaining to a regulator. We hold none of it ourselves, but you're welcome to contact us.

## Children

The App doesn't ask anyone's age and treats every player the same way. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## This website

This website has no cookies or analytics of our own. [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) hosts it and may log technical information such as your IP address.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the date above shows when it last changed. Questions: [support@karellostudio.com](mailto:support@karellostudio.com)
