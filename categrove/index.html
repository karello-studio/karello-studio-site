Categrove

One letter. Fill the grove.

A party word game for friends in the same room. Everyone gets the same letter and races to fill a handful of categories. Answers nobody else thought of score more.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Together in one room

2 to 8 players, each on their own phone. Phones find each other nearby, with no accounts and no internet. Or play solo against the phone.

Seven word languages

Play with words in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch or Portuguese.

Drawn in ink

A quiet grove drawn in ink on paper, by day and by night.

Charlie lives here

Charlie, the studio cat, keeps an eye on every game.
